# License guide

This is a mixed-license collection. There is no single license covering every
asset, record, texture, sound, or script. Including a license text does not assign
that license to an otherwise unlicensed asset.

Use the per-record source, author, license, rights-basis and linked-record fields
in the two catalogs in [docs](docs/), together with the evidence in
[provenance](provenance/). Preserve the original author credits and notices when
redistributing the relevant content. A model's dependent textures and other
components may have their own conditions.

The 3.0 and 4.0 texts are provided for the corresponding Creative Commons license
families. Their inclusion does not establish which version applies to a record.
Use the exact version stated by the source. An unversioned `CC BY` or `CC BY-SA`
entry is not automatically upgraded to 4.0. Preserve any other source-specific
permission or notice; these standard texts do not replace it.

## Scope of project contributions

- The project's original NPC-composition contributions are offered under
  CC0-1.0. The models and equipment used by those NPCs retain their own rights
  and conditions. See the [NPC rights statement](provenance/NPC_Armor_Composition_and_CC0_Rights_Statement.md).
- The newly generated generic replacement textures are attributed to Furkan
  Yaşar and offered under CC0-1.0, within the scope documented in
  [their statement](provenance/cc0/generated_generic_textures_v1_CC0.md).
- The 0 A.D. terrain content is attributed to Wildfire Games under
  CC-BY-SA-3.0. Its [source notice](provenance/licenses/0ad_terrain_LICENSE.txt)
  includes an upstream texture exception and must be retained.
- A `Generic / Commonplace` assessment is a recorded project review, not a
  Creative Commons license or a claim to an unknown third party's rights.
  Likewise, `RESOLVED` is not itself a license grant.
