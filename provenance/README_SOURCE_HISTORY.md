# Source History — v1.0.0

## Ultima IX: Redemption

Ultima IX: Redemption was a cancelled fan project produced by Titans of Ether in the Morrowind engine ecosystem. The historical archive is provenance, not a blanket license; its public availability alone does not grant redistribution rights.

Reference: <https://ultima9.ultimacodex.com/ultima-ix-redemption/>

## OpenMW/UIX-R Libre Edition

OpenMW/UIX-R is the principal upstream manifest and licensing reference: <https://github.com/OpenMW/UIX-R>

Its documented work includes historical asset curation, Bethesda-IP filtering, creator/source distinction, creator contact, Creative Commons permission/relicensing evidence and manifest maintenance.

## OpenMW Free Asset Library recovery

OpenMW Free Asset Library performed a separate recovery and packaging process rather than republishing the historical archive unchanged. Work included record cleanup, dependency analysis, broken-path repair, asset-to-record reconstruction, naming normalization, category organization, creator/license collation, release filtering and repair of limited showcase spaces.

The documented orphan/support subset remains in the current runtime/presentation context and clearly outside the release-approved licensing claim.
